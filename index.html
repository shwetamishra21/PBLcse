HandyHood

Mobile-Based Neighbourhood Community Platform

"An Android application that brings neighbourhood communities together on a single, structured platform — connecting residents, service providers, and admins with a community feed, real-time service request tracking, direct messaging, and role-based access control."

Problem Statement

Modern neighbourhood communities lack a centralised digital platform for communication, service requests, and community management. WhatsApp groups get noisy, notice boards get ignored, and there's no good place to find a service provider your neighbour actually vouched for. HandyHood was built to fix that gap specifically — not communication in general.

Market Research

Analysed existing platforms like Nextdoor and WhatsApp groups. Found gaps in structured service management, role-based access control, lifecycle tracking for service requests, and integrated real-time communication scoped to a neighbourhood.

Innovation

HandyHood provides a unified platform with a multi-role system (Members, Service Providers, Admins), optimistic UI updates, Firestore transaction-safe job acceptance, real-time request lifecycle tracking, typed deep-link notifications, and a Material 3 UI — a complete ecosystem purpose-built for neighbourhood management.

Key Features

Multi-Role System: Members, Service Providers, and Admins with role-specific dashboards and Firestore security rules enforcing access

Community Feed: Posts and admin announcements in a single chronological stream with optimistic likes, comments, and real-time updates

Service Requests: 25+ service types with full lifecycle tracking — Pending → Accepted → In Progress → Completed → Rated

Real-time Messaging: Member-to-Member and Member-to-Provider chats with typing indicators, read receipts, and debounced Firestore writes

Admin Panel: Member management, provider approval, announcement pinning, content moderation, and live community stats

Push Notifications (FCM): Deep-linked notifications carrying a referenceId that route the user directly to the correct screen on tap

Search & Discovery: Three-tab search across Neighbourhoods, Members, and Service Providers with live counts and smart join-state labels

Transaction-Safe Job Acceptance: Firestore transactions prevent double-acceptance race conditions when two providers attempt the same request simultaneously
